## 1. Purpose

This document will define detailed operational behavior for L3 Coherence, Correlation, and Integration.

It extends architectural intent in D01.01 and provides implementation-facing rules for how multi-source observations are associated, reconciled, and converted into bounded state/event outputs.

## 2. Scope

In scope:

- temporal alignment and window policy,
- cross-source association rules,
- duplicate and dependency detection,
- contradiction and conflict-resolution policy,
- confidence and uncertainty aggregation,
- no-state and indeterminate criteria,
- L3 observability and test requirements.

Out of scope:

- L4 semantic interpretation policy,
- L5 orchestration and dispatch channel behavior,
- modality-specific capture contracts (see D01.02).
